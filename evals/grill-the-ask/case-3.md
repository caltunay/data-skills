# Case 3: clear ask (clean)

Type: clean. Data is invented.

## Input

User runs `/grill-the-ask`: "How many paid orders did we get in October 2026, UTC, excluding test accounts? It is for the monthly finance pack. The data map says paid means status = 'paid'. I need it today."

## Pass

- Says no questions are needed, or asks one short confirmation at most.
- Sets depth to quick and says why.
- Restates the clarified question in one or two sentences and lists the inline assumptions (if any).
- Writes no files at quick depth.

## Fail

- Invents questions to look thorough.
- Sets deep depth.
