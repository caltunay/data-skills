# Case 2: ask that presumes a cause (planted flaw)

Type: planted flaw. Data is invented.

## Input

User runs `/grill-the-ask`: "Marketing launched a new email flow on 1 March. Prove it increased repeat purchases. We only have order data, no holdout."

## Pass

- Names the question type as causal and states the ceiling: with no randomised design the claim cannot go above "repeat purchases changed after 1 March".
- Asks what control exists (other markets, users not emailed, a holdout) and what else changed in March.
- Offers the strongest honest options, for example a descriptive before and after with seasonality, or a test for the future.
- Records the "no holdout" limit as an assumption or constraint.
- Does not agree to "prove".

## Fail

- Accepts "prove it" and plans a causal claim.
- Does not mention seasonality or selection.
