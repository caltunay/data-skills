# Mechanistic questions

Ceiling: how exactly X produces Y, step by step. Rarely reachable in business work.

## Short note

- **When it comes up:** "How exactly does the discount lead to more renewals?" The ask wants the full chain, not only the effect.
- **Why it is rarely reachable:** every link in the chain needs its own causal evidence. Business data usually shows the start and the end, not the links.
- **What to do:** flag it. Say: "This is a mechanistic question. The data cannot show the mechanism." Then fall back to the highest type the data supports:
  1. Causal, if a design from the causal method selector fits: "X changed Y by this much." Not how.
  2. Otherwise inferential or exploratory: "X is associated with Y." A mechanism is then a hypothesis.
- **Claim allowed:** the fallback type's claim, plus "one possible explanation is ..., which we did not test".
- **Not allowed:** "X works by ...", "because customers feel ...", any untested chain told as fact.

A funnel or a mediation model does not prove a mechanism. It shows where numbers move, not why.

Get a specialist review before shipping any claim about mechanism.
